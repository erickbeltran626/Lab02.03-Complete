Hacienda Heights, California

Location

Hacienda Heights is an unincorporated community in eastern Los Angeles County, California, about 20 miles east of Downtown Los Agneles. It is included in an area known as the San Gabriel Valley.

About

Community

Hacienda Heights has a diverse suburban community with a strong Asian and Latino demographic

Landmark

It is home to the Hsi Lai Temple, which is one of the largest Buddhist temples in North America.

Landscape

The landscape of Hacienda Heights features a lot of hills, suburban neighborhoods and plentiful nearby hiking trails.

Things To Do

  • Local Dining: Explore the areas local Asian and Mexican cuisine
  • Thomas Burton Park: Relax, picnic or play sports
  • Hacienda Hills Trailhead: Take a hike into the branching trails of Puente Hills
  • Hsi Lai Temple: Explore the massive Buddhist temple and its beautiful gardens
